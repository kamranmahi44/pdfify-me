PDFify Online — Invoice Payment Links
====================================

WHAT A GUEST CAN DO
- Open Invoice Generator without signing up.
- Paste their own PayPal, Wise, and/or Payoneer HTTPS payment link.
- Generate a PDF invoice with clickable “Pay with …” links.

WHAT A VERIFIED ACCOUNT ADDS
- Save the user’s own links privately in their Firebase account.
- Load those links for later invoices and other devices after signing in.
- The Firestore rules limit the payment settings document to the matching verified account UID.

IMPORTANT LIMITATIONS
- PDFify does not receive, process, or store customer card/bank details.
- The button opens the payment provider’s own checkout/payment request page. The customer may still need to choose a method, sign in, and confirm the payment.
- PDFify does not automatically verify payment, mark an invoice as paid, or create amount-specific provider links. For each invoice, use a provider link whose amount and currency match that invoice where supported.
- Provider eligibility, countries, fees, and payment methods vary by provider/account.
- Saving/loading account links requires a configured Firebase project, Email/Password authentication, email verification, Firestore, and the updated firestore.rules published to that project. The package’s placeholder firebase-config.js is not live configuration.

PUBLISH STEPS
1. Upload the package contents to the GitHub Pages repository root; preserve the repository’s existing CNAME file for a custom domain.
2. Configure Firebase using FIREBASE-SETUP.md and replace the placeholder values in firebase-config.js.
3. Publish the updated firestore.rules in the Firebase Console.
4. Test with a verified test account and harmless test payment links before public use.
