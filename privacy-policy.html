Privacy Policy

This policy explains how PDFify Online handles information when you visit the website and use its browser-based tools. It should be updated if the website adds analytics, advertising, user accounts, server-side file processing or other data collection.

Files selected for processing

In the current static version, supported document-processing operations run in your browser. PDFify Online does not intentionally upload selected files to a dedicated PDFify file-processing server. Files and output documents are handled by browser code and downloads are initiated on your device. You are responsible for deciding whether a document is appropriate to process in a browser environment.

Third-party libraries and network requests

The website loads some JavaScript and styling libraries from third-party content delivery networks. Your browser must contact those providers to download the files needed for tools to work. The OCR tool may also download its OCR engine and English language data when you use it. Those requests are governed by the respective providers’ policies. PDFify Online does not control those third-party services.

Cookies, advertising and consent

This website should not claim account features are active until the Firebase configuration has been completed. It does not include a Google publisher ID in this package. If advertising is enabled in the future, Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve, measure or personalize ads as allowed by applicable settings and law. This policy must be reviewed and kept accurate before any advertising or analytics integration goes live. You can learn more about how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

If Google advertising is enabled for visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the site operator must implement the applicable disclosures and consent mechanism, including Google’s requirements for a certified consent management platform where required.

Social profile embeds and links

The site may display Facebook and Instagram profile previews inside the social panel. These previews load only when a visitor opens the panel and may connect the visitor's browser to Meta/Facebook or Instagram. Those providers may receive technical information such as the visitor's IP address and browser data, and may use cookies or similar technologies under their own policies. If a preview does not load, visitors can open the linked profile directly. Following a profile may require signing in to the relevant service. Review Meta's Privacy Policy and Instagram's Privacy Policy.

Contact messages

If you email us, we will receive the information you choose to include in your message, such as your email address and the details of your question. Do not include passwords, full payment details, or confidential document contents in a support email.

Data retention and security

PDFify Online does not offer a user account or a server-side file storage feature in this static version. Your email provider may retain messages sent to us according to its own policies. No website can guarantee that use of a device or network is risk-free; keep your browser up to date and avoid using shared devices for sensitive documents.

Changes to this policy

We may update this page when site functionality or applicable requirements change. The update date at the top indicates the latest review date of this page. For privacy questions, contact info@pdfify.online.

Cloud storage billing

Firebase Cloud Storage requires the Blaze pay-as-you-go billing plan as of October 2026. No-cost allowances may apply, but enabling billing can result in charges based on service usage. Account file storage must remain disabled until the owner understands and configures billing and security rules.
